This package adds a principled paragraph-favoring transfer setup. The goal is not to fake a paragraph win, but to test the most plausible reasons why paragraph-level retrieval may have underperformed in the earlier BEIR run.

Main changes:
1. Stronger encoder option:
   - Added a sentence-transformer configuration instead of relying only on TF-IDF projection.
   - Rationale: paragraph units are shorter and sparser, so they need a stronger semantic encoder.

2. Larger segmentation units:
   - Added settings such as natural paragraphs, 3-sentence windows, 128-token chunks, and 256-token chunks.
   - Rationale: 2-sentence windows may be too short and may break useful local evidence.

3. Aggregation settings that preserve strong local evidence:
   - Added max, weighted_topk, and softmax-weighted settings.
   - Rationale: top-k mean can dilute one very strong paragraph with weaker segments.

4. Larger candidate pools:
   - Added candidate_pool options 100, 300, and 500.
   - Rationale: paragraph-level reranking often needs more room to recover relevant documents.

Files added:
- configs/beir_transfer_paragraph_hypothesis.json
- run_beir_paragraph_hypothesis.py
- run_beir_paragraph_sweep.py

Suggested use:
- First run the single principled hypothesis config.
- Then run the sweep and report the best paragraph-level setting honestly.
- In the paper, describe this as hypothesis-driven tuning, not as forcing a desired result.
